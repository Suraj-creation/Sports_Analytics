# OSNet availability and pre-admission participant-continuity investigation

**An isolated small checkpoint/crop probe is technically feasible; enabling the current OSNet stage alone cannot recover the182withheld athlete rows.** The current model is missing, and appearance extraction happens only after court-admission filters have removed those detections. A positive appearance-supported continuity experiment therefore needs a distinct pre-admission diagnostic path, with its output kept as a target-participant hypothesis rather than a named athlete or scoring authority. No checkpoint was downloaded or loaded, no neural inference/install was run, and no source, model cache, lock, profile, annotation or completed baseline was changed here.

## Proposed bounded diagnostic contract, without threshold fitting

1. **Positive seed only:** initialize a private appearance gallery from uniquely detector-observed, valid current-image **court-origin** participants with existing confidence/motion/ambiguity checks. Never seed from arbitrary officials, the legacy CSV, a predicted roster name or solely a detached blue region. Preserve the original court anchor and source epoch.
2. **Compare before rejection:** on scheduled current detector frames, examine actual raw proposals in their exact image coordinate basis before ROI removal. Use the same pinned embedder/crop normalization. Compare each plausible outside proposal against all live eligible court-origin galleries and competing proposals, using existing cosine0.8and assignment-margin0.05as declared controls. Do not choose a new threshold to fit these182rows.
3. **Preserve the full existing gates:** existing confidence≥0.5, motion/size feasibility, one-to-one assignment, overlap/ambiguous competitor withholding, bounded galleries4/tracks16 and embedder8cropspercall remain explicit. Existing associator additionally treats a match dormant after1s and requires cosine≥0.9with competitor-gallery checks; configured retention is8s. Using0.8alone as an8s reacquisition gate would silently weaken the current policy. Overflow/missing candidates must be explicit unknowns rather than silent truncation.
4. **Separate hypothesis from authority:** emit a typed, frame/epoch/source/model-bound target-participant-continuity hypothesis with seed frame, current observed box, match/runner-up similarities, margins, motion evidence and rejection/abstention reasons. Keep descriptors private. No name, team, scoreboard row, accepted point, physical hitter or pose authority follows automatically. A later policy must explicitly decide whether/how such evidence can maintain participant coverage.
5. **Prevent self-renewal/handover:** keep court-origin provenance and frozen trusted galleries separate from outside continuations; unobserved carries and outside image matches must not rewrite the original court anchor, train the gallery or create an indefinitely self-renewing official/adjacent-court identity. Apply existing retention/reset rules and test occlusion, ambiguous uniforms and appearance drift. Detached scene-memory keys are not stable identity certificates.
6. **Singles/doubles:** retain up to four target athletes and every plausible competitor; near/far and clothing cannot be permanent player/team identities. Same-uniform doubles, close overlaps, two similar galleries, changed ends and unknown format require abstention/capability disclosure. These Test1singles cases alone do not validate doubles.

## Concrete next probe and acceptance evidence

A small isolated download of the exact1,001,683-byte artifact into a **new research-only directory**, with checksum before loading and no shared registry/cache/lock/profile edits, would permit existing-adapter CPU/GPU smoke and bounded crop-similarity diagnostics without installs. That is technically feasible from the verified public artifact; it has **not**been performed. It must be a new experiment, never mixed into the frozen c585baseline or called an unchanged run.

Use exact retained source/proxy frames and **all**actual raw detector competitors from chronological prefixes around the seven withheld intervals, plus confirmed officials/backgrounds, occlusions, genuine edits and unaffected controls from Tests1–2. Seed galleries chronologically before expiry; no future frame, ground-truth point slot or edited-shot appearance leak. Include source6375/6376/8087/14103andTest2official6492/shot-boundary controls. Log every positive/negative/unknown comparison and runtime/memory/checkpoint/device, not selected successful pairs. Compare the existing one-variable controls before considering admission code. First prove missing-candidate coupling, valid crops, finite512-Dfeatures, one-to-one ambiguity and no cross-cut recurrence, with meaningful regression cases for confidence/overlap/capacity/missingweights/expiredseeds; existing unit fake-embedder tests are not real checkpoint or physical accuracy evidence.

There are no independently reviewed athlete IDs/boxes for these sequences. Thus the probe can report hypothesis coverage, source-checked examples, competitor separation and false-authority risks, with physicalIDF1/HOTA/APkeptunavailable. It cannot establish that all182withheld rows should become admitted, nor resolve names/scoreboard/outcomes. The c585candidate remains unaccepted; neither the existing missingmodel nor a successful new appearance probe supplies whole-pipeline acceptance.
