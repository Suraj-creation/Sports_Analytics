A few good
questions.

What kind of footage is suitable?

Recorded badminton matches are the starting point. A stable view, visible court and players, usable lighting and limited occlusion help. Camera movement, cuts and compression can reduce what the system can support. Suitability needs to be assessed for the actual recording.

What can I explore in the product today?

The implemented foundation includes recorded-match playback, frame-linked rally review, match summaries, moments, evidence-linked questions, supported corrections and exports. Shot naming is a rule-based preview. Court movement estimates depend on valid calibration and tracking.

Does this support doubles?

Doubles recordings can be replayed and reviewed at rally and court-side level. Current attribution distinguishes two court-side slots. These do not establish four independent player identities or reliable per-partner statistics.

Is the analysis live?

The current story is about recorded-video analysis. Live and near-real-time operation are development directions that require their own source, latency and reliability validation. Processing time varies with footage and the configured system.

Can it measure speed or biomechanics?

This website makes no validated shuttle-speed, force, precise 3D biomechanics or injury-risk claim. Some court movement can be estimated with calibration and usable tracks; those estimates remain conditional. The demo’s speed question deliberately shows an insufficient-evidence response.

Will the platform expand beyond badminton?

That is the architectural ambition. Other sports and visual workflows can share useful infrastructure, but each needs its own perception models, event rules, evaluation and user experience. They are future opportunities, not available domain products.

About this demonstration

The sports scenes were generated for this website. They depict the idea of preparation, an overhead action and recovery; they are not consecutive frames from an analysed recording.

Movement annotations and the court path are hand-authored illustrations. The example answers are prewritten and their buttons select related scenes. No model is analysing footage here.

The connected product supports recorded-match review and evidence-linked workflows. Tracking, shot labels and movement estimates have source-dependent limits. Precise speed, four-player individual identity and other domains are not demonstrated as validated capabilities.

Data on this site

This standalone website uses locally served fonts, images and scripts. It contains no analytics tracker or third-party form service.

Your demo brief is prepared in the current page. It is not sent, saved to browser storage or uploaded. Copying or downloading is your choice. A server hosting this site may still keep ordinary access logs.

These notes describe this website. The analytics platform’s recording access, retention and consent arrangements must be assessed separately for its deployment.
