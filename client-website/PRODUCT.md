# Rally product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing standalone HTML, CSS and ES modules with Node built-in tooling. No packages, platform imports, API credentials or remote assets are required to deploy the website. Preserve the analytics application.

## Users

Recreational and amateur badminton players who want to relive a rally, understand something in their footage or follow a useful question. Coaches, clubs and platform partners are secondary audiences. Improvement is optional; enjoyment is valuable.

## Product Purpose

Turn ordinary video into a structured, inspectable record, then into useful understanding. Badminton is the first concrete product. The broader ambition is an evidence-grounded video-intelligence platform with independently validated domain modules.

## Positioning

Perception supplies observations. Temporal logic assembles events. Defined calculations supply numbers. The agentic layer retrieves and explains evidence rather than inventing match facts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Recorded-match playback, rally review, deterministic summaries, evidence-linked questions, supported corrections and exports are established product areas. Shot labels and movement depend on source quality and documented limitations. P1/P2 labels do not establish four-player doubles or persistent identity. No validated shuttle-speed, biomechanical force, injury-risk, universal live or other-sport capability is claimed.

The local checkout and prior server audit have different maturity. A polished public illustration must not turn a proposed validation gate or domain interface into a delivered guarantee. The metric catalogue will use one explicitly synthetic six-rally fixture; its numbers are calculated, not asserted as customer results.

## Brand Commitments

Rally is the approved working name. Evolve the existing identity: forest/ivory/lime, directional energy, simple editable geometry. Preserve original logo assets. The user explicitly requested internal exploration before selecting the strongest direction, a much less repetitive image system, and more visible pose/analytics content.

## Evidence on Hand

Complete master context and creative brief in Design/references; existing engine, analytics and agent source; previous website and claim register. User-supplied video-111.mp4 is a 17-second basketball broadcast/tracking reference, not proof of a shipped Rally basketball product. Generated badminton scenes are illustrations. No customer logos, adoption data, testimonials, commercial pricing or accuracy benchmark is supplied.

## Product Principles

- Personal value before analytical complexity.
- Numbers come from defined source records.
- Every important answer has a route back to evidence.
- Estimates, missing inputs and future capabilities are visible.
- New domains require their own models, rules and validation.

## Operating Context

This revision is local at the user's explicit request. Work in the Windows repository's client-website folder; do not connect to or change the GPU server. Preserve other user changes. Deployment remains a later, separate operation.
