## Pose estimation

YOLO11n-pose ran **offline on the local CPU** using already installed Ultralytics/PyTorch, with image size 960. The model detected the principal person in each generated sports image. The 17 COCO-format keypoints were exported as image coordinates and confidence values in src/pose-data.js. The site omits points below 0.35 confidence and any edge attached to an omitted point.

The hero has 16 visible points; the overhead scene has 15. These are actual model estimates on generated images, with possible joint-location errors. No model runs in the browser. The model, weights, Python environment and packages are not shipped. This is a visual asset preparation step, not an integration of pose into the sports analytics platform.

Single-image 2D pose does not establish stroke classification, calibrated movement, shuttle speed, technique quality, injury risk or biomechanical validity. The dotted movement paths, court positions and event labels are explicitly illustrative and hand-authored.

Official model documentation: [Ultralytics pose tasks](https://docs.ultralytics.com/tasks/pose/). Review the model provider's terms for any future model integration; the website contains only derived point coordinates and original images, not the model software.

## New image direction

The net-recovery prompt requested a low three-quarter courtside view of an adult South Asian man in an ivory shirt and charcoal shorts, reaching forward in a plausible low net lunge, whole body and single racket visible, forest-green indoor badminton hall, realistic anatomy, documentary light, no text, logos, UI or baked-in analytics.

The after-game prompt requested a warm candid 4:3 courtside view of two different amateur players, a man in his fifties and a woman in her thirties, looking at a phone together after playing, different wardrobes and body positions, rackets nearby, real indoor court context, no branding or UI.

No external site artwork, famous athlete likeness, customer recording or reference-site logo was copied. Existing video reuse rights must be confirmed by the owner before public deployment; this local revision does not publish it.

## Data separation

src/catalogue-data.js is a hand-authored six-rally record with two excluded warm-up exchanges. It is not measured from any displayed scene or the supplied video. Charts, guided answers, definitions, highlights and inspectable event sequences all use that same fixture. A citation opens its actual synthetic record, not an unrelated image.
