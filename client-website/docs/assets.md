## Overlay and demonstration contract

Pose lines, point coordinates, court path and phase selections are hand-authored SVG/HTML. The original scenes remain visible. The UI labels these as illustrated annotations, not inferred measurements. Source/evidence controls change real page state, but do not call an analysis API. Prewritten answers explain the concept and include a deliberate insufficient-evidence state.

The generated scenes have plausible sports context but are not a biomechanical reference or consecutive samples from a real action. Future replacement with genuine footage needs appropriate reuse rights and participant permissions.
