# Claim and evidence register

Reviewed 29 October 2026. This register explains the website's language. It is not a model-accuracy certificate.

## Current local revision

The revision is entirely local and did not reconnect to the GPU server. The historical implementation evidence below was gathered for the earlier build; it is not a fresh certification of the current platform. The local checkout has older integration notes and still lists tracking jitter, missing quality gates and badminton-specific API/agent assumptions as gaps. The revised public copy therefore describes direction and scope without claiming that every proposed calibration or evidence gate is universally enforced.

The website itself implements confidence-filtered 2D pose display from offline estimates on generated scenes; a deterministic six-rally analytics fixture; real fixture records behind citations; six catalogue views; a bounded question demo; native reference-video playback; and local brief preparation. It does not upload/analyse visitor footage or call an AI backend.

Movement paths and event annotations are illustrative. Progress is explicitly planned. The supplied basketball clip is an annotated reference, not a Rally output. Existing read-only platform tools informed the story; the browser demo simulates their retrieval steps with local records and identifies its final check as local.

| Other sports/workflows | A reusable foundation is the architectural ambition | New domains need their own models, rules, validation, permissions and product design |

## Explicit exclusions

No fabricated customers, testimonials, business scale, benchmark accuracy, cost savings or processing-time claims. No validated shuttle speed, injury prediction, precise 3D biomechanics, universal camera support, full live service, guaranteed improvement, persistent athlete identity or all-four-player doubles attribution is promised.

Geometric court-isolation exclusion is not labelled shuttle, contact, event or shot accuracy. Historical broadcast results and hardware FPS figures are not reused as current public proof.

The site makes narrow, verifiable statements about its own data behavior. It does not turn proposed platform consent, retention, deletion or sharing policies into current guarantees.
