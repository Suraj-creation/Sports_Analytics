# External repositories: adoption analysis against our pipeline

Snapshot: 2 November 2026. This extends [the prior source-level report](../../research/EXTERNAL_BADMINTON_REPOSITORIES_ANALYSIS.md) with direct comparisons to the current checkout (baseline commit `32a8295d9645d81bd1586a777541bb2f72c08bed`). It is a static review: no weights, datasets or videos were downloaded, no inference or tests were run, and none of the external authors' metrics were reproduced. Local source/metadata inventories were inspected at the three requested pinned revisions. External file links below point to those revisions; local links identify the corresponding baseline code.

## Decision overview

| Area | External insight | Existing project | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doubles association | J jointly matches at most two players per half using metric prediction and a sticky side. | `PersistentPlayerAssociator` already performs global assignment with appearance, motion, explicit dummy/unknown matches and ambiguity handling. | **Benchmark** court-distance information on labelled crossings; do not replace the current associator or force cold fills. |
| Contacts / hitter | J offers raw-gap, motion-onset and wrist-speed candidate passes. | Multi-cue contact fusion already uses non-interpolated shuttle samples, normalized player proximity, wrist motion and optional observed racket overlap. | **Benchmark** additional serve/gap proposals as uncertain candidates; do not label them contacts without evaluation. |
| Rally / score | J segments from broadcast wide-view runs and can use scoreboard-driven gap rescue. | Contact/motion rally FSM and scoreboard reconciliation already exist, with replay and unresolved-state handling. | **Benchmark** score-assisted recall on broadcast recordings; avoid using the wide-view proxy as a universal rally detector. |
| Shuttle reconstruction | D preserves stage-by-stage intermediate fields and applies bounded gap repair plus offline RTS smoothing. | `Track` already carries `interp`; gap filling uses both anchors and look-ahead. | **Adapt diagnostics**, benchmark bounded/event-aware reconstruction; retain observed versus reconstructed semantics. |
| Calibration | D presents named calibration and residual review. | Our named court landmarks, RANSAC fit, painted-line support, court tracking and calibration lifecycle already exist. | **Adapt validation protocol** with held-out landmarks and per-region uncertainty, not a wholesale calibrator replacement. |
| Shot display | V's hosted per-frame detector matches class boxes to player boxes. | Rule and optional BST adapters classify at candidate-event context, not via V's service. | **Avoid** treating per-frame labels as contacts or copying V code. |
| Evaluation | J explicitly warns reviewed samples may be biased; its hit matcher allows a frame tolerance. | Versioned interval evaluation and shot/contact benchmarks already distinguish detection and conditional labels. | **Adapt** J's review-provenance warning and held-out declarations; measure all missed events as well as matched labels. |

All recommendations are research hypotheses; no improvement in our footage is established. Consult [architecture and ownership](../architecture/full-pipeline-architecture.md) before changing shared schemas.

## J — BadmintonAI

Pinned source: [`5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b`](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/tree/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b).

**Entry points and data.** The [singles driver](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/scripts/run_match_chain.sh) runs player parsing, tracking validation, shuttle tracking, `pipeline` label-free strokes, scoreboard construction and web export. Its shell paths are author-specific. [`pipeline.rally_windows` / `build_strokes` / `classify`](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/src/badminton/pipeline.py) consume stored frame-indexed tracks and shuttle records; default windows can come from ShuttleSet labels, while `--label-free` uses segmentation and optionally scoreboard-based rescue. The classifier trains on labelled matches and may fall back. Nonfinal `landing` features use the next hitter's feet, not observed shuttle landings. Do not interpret precomputed dashboards or labelled windows as fully automatic results.

**Association comparison.** [`_SlotState`, `_best_matching` and `SlotAssigner.update`](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/src/badminton/doubles/track.py) retain the two largest detections per court half, preserve raw-ID slot ownership, extrapolate last metric velocity with actual frame delta, and solve leftover slots jointly. Base radius is 1.5 m, grows with predicted travel to 4 m, and expires after 15 frames; 0.35 m/frame is a velocity cap. A five-frame sticky-half policy prevents single-frame flips, while 600 frames expire raw-ID side memory. Its finite `COLD_COST=100` and cardinality-first matching may assign a weakly supported candidate; fresh process chunks recreate state. [`doubles.identity`](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/src/badminton/doubles/identity.py) separately maps named players via per-set manual quadrant anchors. Geometric formation roles do not prove persistent personal identity.

Our [`PersistentPlayerAssociator.update`](../../sports/badminton/src/bai_badminton/perception/player_association.py) already calls `linear_sum_assignment` with dummy columns for withholding, combines image-foot motion and optional 512-D neural appearance, expires hypotheses and withholds ambiguous overlaps or close alternatives. Its unobserved rows do not create identities or update appearance. [`IdentityResolver.assign`](../../sports/badminton/src/bai_badminton/identity.py) and [`IdentityReviewLedger`](../../sports/badminton/src/bai_badminton/identity_review.py) separately address participant binding and review. These algorithms trade recall for abstention; they are not proof of correct identity. A useful ablation is adding or evaluating a **gated metric cue** only when target-court calibration is valid, preserving abstention. Compare labelled ID switches, partner attribution, identity coverage and unresolved rates on crossings, occlusions, cuts and calibration loss. J's projected-metre thresholds can amplify a wrong or moving calibration; a given frame count also corresponds to different durations at different FPS.

**Contacts and rallies.** [`hits.detect_hits`](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/src/badminton/hits.py) combines trajectory change and turn candidates, boundary velocities around raw visibility gaps, serve motion onset, and wrist-speed peaks near raw shuttle evidence. It uses fixed frame/pixel thresholds and nearest-wrist/bbox attribution. [`doubles.strokes.contacts` and `enforce_alternation`](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/src/badminton/doubles/strokes.py) instead propose extrema of image y and remove consecutive same-side candidates; same-side nearest-player matching can default to a slot. Image-y extrema are camera-dependent and can be flight apexes; enforced alternation is not an independent validation of contact detection.

Our [`score_frame` / `detect_contacts`](../../sports/badminton/src/bai_badminton/events/contact.py) combine redirect, impulse, normalized proximity and pose swing, optionally using observed racket overlap; `score_frame` requires an observed shuttle at the candidate frame (`vis & ~interp`). Ambiguous player proximity can produce an unresolved hitter. These are still uncalibrated heuristics. J's raw-gap and serve-onset branches target potentially missed events when the shuttle is absent or begins moving, but should be introduced only as explicitly uncertain candidate evidence and benchmarked independently for false positives and temporal error, not silently promoted to observed contacts.

[`segment.segments`](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/src/badminton/segment.py) uses both-player tracking and bbox height as a broadcast-camera proxy plus hit groups; [`score_seq.repair` / `complete`](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/src/badminton/score_seq.py) repair score progression. Our [`RallyFSM`](../../sports/badminton/src/bai_badminton/events/rally_fsm.py) uses contact, shuttle visibility/motion, replay suppression, merge and delayed finalization, while [`ScoreReconciler`](../../sports/badminton/src/bai_badminton/score_ocr.py) uses stable readings and board deltas. Neither source proves event accuracy. A static amateur camera can show two players between rallies, so J's wide-camera heuristic is not transferable without a domain-specific ablation. Score deltas corroborate a rally result; they do not establish contact timing.

**Provenance and evaluation.** [`shuttle.import_csv`](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/src/badminton/shuttle.py) inserts frame, coordinates and visibility, despite the module docstring's claim that repaired rows receive an interpolation flag. Treat that particular import's reconstruction provenance as unavailable. [`hits.validate`](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/src/badminton/hits.py) performs one-to-one match-by-tolerance against labelled windows; [`review_eval`](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/src/badminton/review_eval.py) validates held-out declarations and explicitly warns that selected reviewed events are not whole-match accuracy. Our [`evaluate_intervals`](../../engine/src/bai_engine/evaluation.py) uses ordered one-to-one matching and reports `None` for missing denominators; [`shot_benchmark.evaluate`](../../research/shot_benchmark.py) reports detection and family metrics, and [`benchmark_reference_session._source_binding`](../../research/evaluation/benchmark_reference_session.py) checks media and annotation provenance. These support evaluation but cannot manufacture ground truth. Review samples anchored only on predictions omit false-negative contacts.

## D — badminton

Pinned source: [`1690934989ff3b64e61fcc497518bb652f8d0b5f`](https://github.com/Dinil007/badminton/tree/1690934989ff3b64e61fcc497518bb652f8d0b5f).

**Phase-based execution.** [`main.py`](https://github.com/Dinil007/badminton/blob/1690934989ff3b64e61fcc497518bb652f8d0b5f/main.py) dispatches separate phases for player tracking, pose, multiple shuttle variants, line calling and visualization; default phase 9.1 is not a full preprocessing pipeline. Intermediate CSVs are prerequisites, and code contains clip-specific Windows paths and defaults. [`RallyDetector._analyze_rallies`](https://github.com/Dinil007/badminton/blob/1690934989ff3b64e61fcc497518bb652f8d0b5f/src/analytics/rally.py) creates one rally from known frames 140 and 809 at 50 fps. Empty court/visualization files are not implementations.

**Trajectory.** [`TemporalEnsemble.ensemble_frame`](https://github.com/Dinil007/badminton/blob/1690934989ff3b64e61fcc497518bb652f8d0b5f/src/shuttle/temporal_ensemble.py) thresholds candidates from overlapping eight-frame windows, rejects points far from the coordinate median, then weights position by temporal window position and squared confidence. If all candidates are rejected it falls back to the highest-confidence candidate, which can be unsafe for multiple nearby shuttles. [`AdvancedShuttleTracker.process_tracking`](https://github.com/Dinil007/badminton/blob/1690934989ff3b64e61fcc497518bb652f8d0b5f/src/shuttle/advanced_tracker.py) applies court/kinematic filtering, bounded gap repair and bidirectional [RTS smoothing](https://github.com/Dinil007/badminton/blob/1690934989ff3b64e61fcc497518bb652f8d0b5f/src/shuttle/rts_smoother.py), retaining raw, ensemble, rejection, inpaint and final fields. [`find_bounded_gaps`](https://github.com/Dinil007/badminton/blob/1690934989ff3b64e61fcc497518bb652f8d0b5f/src/shuttle/inpaint.py) requires two support observations on both sides and defaults to gaps of six frames or fewer. **Artifact caveat:** `repair_gap_inpaintnet` loads the network before checking the short-window or invalid-output spline fallbacks; a missing checkpoint is not automatically handled by those fallbacks in that path. V3 and InpaintNet checkpoints are absent from the inventoried tree.

Our [`Track.from_arrays`, `fill_gaps` and `process`](../../sports/badminton/src/bai_badminton/perception/shuttle_post.py) already filter spikes, gate internal gaps by length, both-side anchors, implied speed and motion, and mark synthesized positions `interp=True`; the internal `vis` mask is also set true for interpolation. [`contact.score_frame`](../../sports/badminton/src/bai_badminton/events/contact.py) explicitly excludes `interp`, but all persisted/exported consumers should be checked rather than equating `vis` with a directly visible shuttle. D's stage diagnostics motivate recording original/accepted/reconstructed observations and rejection reason separately. Future-looking smoothing must be offline and contact-aware; neither a smooth curve nor a raw-versus-smoothed displacement proves a detection is correct.

**Calibration and landing.** [`calibration.compute_homography` / `validate_homography`](https://github.com/Dinil007/badminton/blob/1690934989ff3b64e61fcc497518bb652f8d0b5f/src/court/calibration.py) fit image pixels to court metres from named clicks, using RANSAC for more than four points and reporting residuals. Our [`court.fit_homography`](../../sports/badminton/src/bai_badminton/court.py) fits in the opposite direction (court to image), and [`CourtFit`, `select_primary`, `CourtTracker`](../../sports/badminton/src/bai_badminton/perception/court_lines.py) combine line support, geometry and temporal tracking. A measured residual on fitted points is not independent held-out accuracy, and RANSAC thresholds have different units in the two coordinate directions. Compare held-out court landmarks and residuals by region; keep calibration loss explicit.

D's [`inspect_landing_sequence`](https://github.com/Dinil007/badminton/blob/1690934989ff3b64e61fcc497518bb652f8d0b5f/src/line_calling/detector.py) uses a fixed final-frame window and selects the last detected/reacquired/predicted point. Its separate [`ContactDetector`](https://github.com/Dinil007/badminton/blob/1690934989ff3b64e61fcc497518bb652f8d0b5f/src/line_calling/contact_detector.py) combines approach, vertical change, rebound, proximity and confidence, but its implementation includes a clip-specific image baseline. Neither establishes a general ground-contact detector or line-call accuracy. Our [`analyze_rally_end`](../../sports/badminton/src/bai_badminton/events/rally_outcome.py) also produces heuristic outcomes including `UNKNOWN`; neither path justifies officiating-grade claims.

## V — Badminton-VisionAI

Pinned source: [`a17e47251b971aa7fda6d60d0424ffb3887030b6`](https://github.com/amramer/Badminton-VisionAI/tree/a17e47251b971aa7fda6d60d0424ffb3887030b6).

[`app.main`](https://github.com/amramer/Badminton-VisionAI/blob/a17e47251b971aa7fda6d60d0424ffb3887030b6/app.py) reads all video frames into memory, obtains cached or fixed first-frame court points, batches player detections, tracks shuttle coordinates and renders overlays/metrics; the Streamlit viewer consumes saved outputs. [`PlayerTracker.run_tracker`](https://github.com/amramer/Badminton-VisionAI/blob/a17e47251b971aa7fda6d60d0424ffb3887030b6/tracking/players_tracking/player_tracking.py) uses YOLO person detection, a court polygon, ByteTrack and a two-box mean; `Player.assign_player` assigns one of two public IDs by image-side of net rather than persistent four-person identity. [`BallTracker.run_tracker`](https://github.com/amramer/Badminton-VisionAI/blob/a17e47251b971aa7fda6d60d0424ffb3887030b6/tracking/shuttle_tracking/ball_tracking.py) uses eight-frame TrackNet windows, weighted overlap and optional InpaintNet, with external configured checkpoints.

[`ShotDetector.run` / `_get_primary_shot`](https://github.com/amramer/Badminton-VisionAI/blob/a17e47251b971aa7fda6d60d0424ffb3887030b6/shot_detection/detector.py) send each annotated frame to a hosted Roboflow detector, select its highest-confidence class box and match the box by IoU to a player. No shuttle trajectory or physical contact drives the call; adjacent frame records are not necessarily distinct shots. Exception and empty-shot branches have static initialization risks; no failure was reproduced. The configuration includes a credential-shaped value: do not copy it or assume it is valid. Our [`StrokeClassifier`](../../sports/badminton/src/bai_badminton/events/stroke.py) accepts a contact context and can return `unknown`; [`BST.prepare_sequence`](../../sports/badminton/src/bai_badminton/events/bst.py) requires observed two-player pose evidence and is not evidence of doubles generalization. Per-frame V labels should not become ground-truth contacts or bypass attribution uncertainty.

[`Metrics.into_dataframe`](https://github.com/amramer/Badminton-VisionAI/blob/a17e47251b971aa7fda6d60d0424ffb3887030b6/analysis/metrics.py) derives ball velocity and a `ball_shot_power1` from projected planar coordinates. An airborne shuttle is not on the floor plane, so this does not establish physical speed or shot power. The useful idea is separating viewer/report from processing and carrying frame-aligned outputs; our engine/store and API already provide that separation. Avoid whole-video memory growth and stale cache-by-file-existence behavior.

## Evaluation proposal and acceptance criteria

1. **Freeze a comparable baseline.** Record code and model revisions/hashes, selected court, calibration, proxy dimensions/FPS and source identity, and match-level development versus held-out split. Use the same labelled windows for both implementations. Explicitly mark missing individual-identity, shuttle, impact and floor-contact labels as unavailable.
2. **Doubles assignment ablation.** Compare the unchanged local associator with an isolated metric-cue experiment on annotated four-player crossings, short and long occlusions, similar clothing and camera/calibration loss. Report ID switches, partner-attribution precision/recall, identity coverage and unresolved rate. Retain ambiguity when evidence is insufficient; no forced assignment solely to improve coverage.
3. **Contact ablation.** Evaluate raw-gap and serve-onset proposals separately from the local baseline. Report precision/recall, false contacts per minute, timing distribution, side and player attribution on every labelled contact, including misses. Compare singles and doubles separately and do not count interpolated locations as direct observations.
4. **Trajectory ablation.** Compare current gap filling to bounded and contact-aware alternatives with observed and deliberately masked labelled points; stratify by gap length, court, shuttle size, candidate overlap and proximity to contact. Measure localization error, visibility precision/recall and discontinuity retention. Offline RTS must be reported separately from causal output. Review inferred points as inferred, even if numerically accurate.
5. **Calibration and score.** Use held-out manually labelled landmarks and painted-line support, split by court region and camera; separately evaluate rally boundaries and OCR agreement against independently reviewed intervals. A score or geometric exclusion metric is not an event-accuracy metric.
6. **Gate adoption.** Require reproducible improvement on the same held-out footage, acceptable regressions, verified artifact rights, explicit schema/cache compatibility and tests on the authorized runtime. If labels, weights, compute or permissions are unavailable, report the result as unavailable rather than claiming improvement.
