# BadmintonAI contact, perception and format study — 3 November 2026

## Scope and decision

The external implementation offers useful **candidate-generation ideas**, especially short visibility-gap analysis, observed wrist-motion recovery and an explicit serve-onset pass. It does not supply a trained racket-contact model or establish physical contact from images. Its strongest headline hit metrics are measured inside labelled rally windows and cannot be compared directly with our unrestricted whole-video event output. Retain the existing baseline and evidence safeguards while evaluating bounded additional cues through replaceable badminton components.

The actual external doubles source is more developed than its README and some sections of `DOUBLES.md`: it builds four-slot strokes and shot summaries. However, doubles contacts use image-y extrema, inferred side, same-side nearest wrist/box, and forced alternation. There are no independently labelled doubles contact/hitter results in the inspected material. These are research estimates, not a ready replacement for our four-participant contact path.

No external source was executed or imported into production during this study. No local test, model inference or media processing was run. GPU SSH was temporarily unavailable during the initial investigation; the integration owner subsequently reconnected and owns remote execution. Execution results are not assumed before that owner reports them. Original local modifications and untracked directories were preserved.

## What the external contact chain actually executes

The complete inspected [hit implementation](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/src/badminton/hits.py) has four heuristic passes. It does not run a racket detector.

| Pass | Actual input and rule | Output and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Velocity change / reversal | Interpolated shuttle image coordinates. Incoming/outgoing finite differences span four frames each. Candidate score is the maximum of normalized velocity change and a turn signal; velocity threshold 30 px/frame, turn threshold 20, minimum turn about 60 degrees. | Greedy score-ranked suppression with an eight-frame minimum gap. Thresholds depend on source resolution and frame rate. A point may already be reconstructed. |
| Visibility-gap recovery | Two three-sample raw visible runs bracket a gap of 2–14 frame indices. Compare velocity before and after that gap with the same signal. | Assigns the integer midpoint frame and mean endpoint image coordinates, tagged `in_gap=True`. This is a timing/location reconstruction, not an observed shuttle sample or proof that a contact occurred in the gap. |
| Serve onset | First three consecutive raw shuttle steps each at least 6 px/frame, separated from existing peaks. | Tags the start as `serve=True`. The function operates in a supplied rally window and does not itself prove service formation, server identity or match intent. |
| Wrist recovery | COCO wrists with confidence above .3; speed at least 10 px/frame; some raw shuttle sample within 60 px over a five-frame neighbourhood. | Adds `wrist=True` candidates outside existing suppression ranges. A fast preparation or fake can pass. Speed pairs the closest of either wrist at the two endpoints, which can switch anatomical hands; it is not a same-wrist velocity measurement. |

Attribution searches image-space wrist/box references at candidate frame ±1 for `near`, `near2`, `far`, `far2`. It chooses the smallest distance without a maximum-distance rejection, runner-up ambiguity margin, racket-side observation or abstention on equal candidates. Missing wrist confidence falls back to the stored YOLO box centre. The `hits.py` docstring retains older near/far language while the function now enumerates all four slots. Near/far slots are court-side observations, not durable player identities.

The [player detector](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/src/badminton/detect.py) uses YOLO11x-Pose at image size 1280 with bundled ByteTrack. Singles retains the largest in-court detection per half. A ground-point heuristic blends ankle y with bbox-bottom y by .65 and takes lateral x from available ankles. Its measured floor-foot error does not establish airborne shuttle geometry, racket position or four-player identity.

The [shuttle adapter](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/src/badminton/shuttle.py) invokes vendored TrackNetV3 with weighted temporal ensembling and an InpaintNet checkpoint. It monkeypatches Torch device/load and DataLoader globally for MPS/CPU. This is not equivalent to our configured eight-frame non-overlap TrackNet runtime and is unsuitable as an in-process host adapter. Preserve our explicit device/runtime contracts rather than copying these patches.

There is a material provenance mismatch: `shuttle.py`'s introductory comment promises marking repaired InpaintNet rows, but `import_csv` inserts only `(match_id, frame_num, img_x, img_y, visible)`. No repaired/observed flag reaches the table through this insert. Consequently `hits.shuttle_series(interpolate=False)` means no *additional pandas interpolation*; it does not prove every point originated from a visible image heatmap rather than InpaintNet. Our `vis & ~interp` distinction must not be removed to imitate its recall.

## What the published numbers measure

The README reports four broadcast singles matches and designates India Open as tuning and Denmark Open as held out. These are the authors' results, not reproduced experiments here. Their source is clearer than the headline table alone:

- `hits.validate` obtains human contact frames, then runs detection separately in each labelled rally's first-to-last contact window padded by 20 frames. This removes most unlabelled dead time before counting false positives. Matching is greedy one-to-one within ±6 video frames. At 30 fps that permits .2 s error, much wider than one physical impact frame.
- Label-to-video alignment is configured per match (`ss_shuttle_offset`: India −1, Denmark −3). Player validation has a separately selected alignment. These offsets concern label/video alignment, not a free shift between actual player and shuttle observations.
- Hitter agreement is conditional on a matched contact and both available predicted reference and label-side map. Unknown references or maps are omitted from the attribution denominator. It is not end-to-end named-player recall and does not penalize every missed contact.
- Landing evaluation uses only eligible floor-ending labelled rallies with an available estimate. A median on this subset does not evaluate every rally or verify airborne measurements.

The dated [accuracy report](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/docs/ACCURACY_IMPROVEMENTS.md) explicitly distinguishes detector-in-labelled-window validation from saved whole-chain contacts. Its baseline table reports contact coverage around 85–89% but contact precision only about 67–79% across the four matches. Its wrist-neighbourhood experiment reports additional true contacts **and** additional false positives. Do not transfer those dated table values to a later exported snapshot without checking the exact generating revision and definitions. The separate shot/evaluation study owns current exported snapshot comparisons.

The newer [review evaluator](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/src/badminton/review_eval.py) improves reproducibility: explicit training/held-out declarations, exact player-name remapping and unique-nearest ±6-frame matching with ties uncredited. However, reviews are anchored to existing AI contacts. Unless the review protocol separately enumerates missed contacts and non-contact spans, that review population cannot establish full contact recall or false-positive rate. It supplements an exhaustive independent label set.

## Actual external doubles behavior and failure modes

The full [doubles stroke source](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/src/badminton/doubles/strokes.py) exists despite stale README/deferred-work descriptions. It says the two-dimensional nearest-wrist singles path mis-sided its broadcast doubles example and therefore uses a different detector:

1. It reads the shuttle trajectory, then calls `np.interp` across remaining missing values and through leading/trailing missing spans. The bounded six-frame interpolation of the shared helper is therefore no longer the final gap bound for this detector.
2. A three-frame moving average and extrema prominence of at least 15 px, or 12% of the whole-window image-y span, propose near maxima and far minima at seven-frame separation.
3. It collapses adjacent same-side candidates to the more extreme y. Post-collapse alternation is 100% by construction. Raw alternation is a useful consistency diagnostic, not a contact precision/recall or correct-side metric: missed even numbers of hits, alternating false peaks and long gaps can preserve it.
4. It picks the nearest same-side wrist/box over ±1 frame. If no reference exists, `attribute` assigns the side's first slot. That forced partner identity is incompatible with this project's evidence contract.
5. It uses the next attributed hitter's feet as a landing proxy; the terminal flight uses `hits.find_landing`. A target athlete's floor location is not an observed shuttle landing.
6. It trains its doubles shot baseline on labelled India Open **singles** geometry and marks it as geometry in the intermediate DataFrame. This is transferred inference, not a doubles-validated classifier.

Image-y extrema are camera-specific cues. A shuttle can turn vertically at a flight apex, an image-height extremum can shift under a camera pan, and an overhead/rear-court contact need not be an unambiguous near/far extremum. These physical limitations follow from the rule's inputs; they have not been quantified on our footage. Adopt only as a named optional shadow cue with camera/scene validity and preserved uncertainty, not a side oracle or a replacement for four-participant evidence.

The full [doubles tracker](https://github.com/jayclim/BadmintonAI/blob/5c356f05cde0ed90c75316c133e0aba495e5232b/src/badminton/doubles/track.py) retains top-two detections per half and assigns slots through ByteTrack persistence, short-gap constant-velocity prediction, a gap-scaled distance radius and joint two-by-two assignment. These are useful ideas for track hypotheses. Its assignment always prefers maximum cardinality, allows cold fill when re-identification fails, suppresses smaller extra people, and retains sticky half/slot state. This does not prove physical identity. Our current episode births, reset/ambiguity events, review intervals and explicit unresolved IDs are stronger provenance controls and must survive any replacement. Neither implementation automatically establishes named identities from four same-uniform players.

### Read-only diagnosis: detector cadence and bracketing geometry

The integration owner subsequently reported Hylo's first `visual_tracker_reset` ambiguity event at frame 203, fifteen such events overall, and a 676-contact participant-attribution split of **400 `player_not_detector_matched`, 269 `nearest_proximity`, seven ambiguous**. Raw/retracted payloads form a different population and must not be added to these current-contact totals. No fresh recount or media review was performed by this study author.

The inspected source explains how a valid contact candidate can reach the 400-case veto:

1. `PlayerPerception.process` skips person detection on a strided frame. `_carry` preserves the last box, decays its support, explicitly sets `detector_matched=False`, and may still run current-frame pose according to its separate schedule. The frame's `detector_observed` remains false. On a scheduled detector frame, ByteTrack can also return an unmatched predicted box, producing a distinct `detector_observed=True, detector_matched=False` case.
2. Engine `_append` preserves both flags in `ParticipantSnapshot`. `participant_window` accepts the box and available current pose as contact-scoring context; it withholds the racket when the person is unmatched. Consequently `score_frame` can propose a trajectory/body-supported contact at a carried frame. This is an estimated contact, not a false claim that the box was freshly detected.
3. `_advance` preserves the contact while named actors may already be withheld by the identity ambiguity latch. `_on_contact` separately calls `participant_contact_evidence`; that function ranks episodes with `score_frame`, then nulls the winning episode/track/birth IDs when its **exact current frame** has `detector_matched is False`, returning `player_not_detector_matched`.
4. `_classify` requires a provisional episode hypothesis and current `detector_observed=True`, as well as birth/track agreement. Its anonymous singles path additionally requires exactly two unique current target-court episodes and a detector run for both. Carried-frame geometry therefore cannot enter this path. `RuleStrokeClassifier` withholds a non-serve class without hitter court position, and also independently requires post-contact observed speed, valid flight duration and resolved initial-contact context.

Thus 400 is a **freshness-veto count**, not a measured missed-hitter count or an automatically recoverable classifier population. Before changing it, the GPU owner should split those cases by frame-level detector execution (false versus true versus unknown), locate nearest fresh same-episode brackets and their temporal distance, and join each contact to its canonical stroke's abstention reason. Some cases will still lack speed/flight/service context or have ambiguous partners. The 269 nearest-proximity cases can also have null named actors after a reset without being geometry failures.

A bounded estimate can supply provisional geometry for an existing contact on an **unscheduled detector frame**, provided the same episode is observed freshly on both sides. Do not implement this by setting current detector flags true, manufacturing a `PersonObs`/`ParticipantSnapshot`, copying an old racket into the current frame, or restoring a name. Use a separate derived geometry result. The initial scope should reject a scheduled detector run that failed to match the person, rather than silently treating that negative/ambiguous evidence like a planned skipped run.

The existing narrow entry points are `participant_contact_evidence(window, frames, index, *, restrict_target_court=False) -> dict` and `participant_window(window, frames, *, restrict_target_court=False) -> WindowObs`. Neither carries the engine's raw visibility-count/negative-identity masks. An adjacent helper can reuse the same aligned input arrays while requiring those masks explicitly; it should not change the observation-building functions:

```python
participant_contact_geometry(
    window: WindowObs,
    frames: list[dict[str, ParticipantSnapshot]],
    index: int,
    *,
    match_format: Literal["singles", "doubles"],
    geometry_valid: NDArray[np.bool_],
    identity_unresolved: NDArray[np.bool_],
    visible_counts: NDArray[np.int64],
    max_bracket_s: float = 0.2,
) -> ParticipantGeometryResult
```

This is a **proposed signature**, not implemented source. The maximum span is an explicit tunable support bound, not a calibrated error guarantee; six CFR frames at 30 fps illustrates its cadence scope. An engine owner may group existing masks in its typed component input instead of adding parallel arguments. Missing masks must not default to valid or identity-resolved. The output needs `status=estimate|unresolved`, provisional episode/track/birth IDs, canonical query frame, original **absolute** `source_frames`, `position_kind=estimated`, separate optional `bbox_px_estimate`/`court_xy_m_estimate`, method `same_episode_detector_bracket_v1`, configuration identity and a rejection reason. It must not contain a named actor assignment. Source detector observations remain distinct from the derived result.

Required gates for an initial conservative implementation:

- Require a current carried episode with explicitly false detector-execution flag; nearest left and right anchors must each have `detector_observed is True` **and** `detector_matched is True`, the same nonnegative track ID and the same nonempty episode birth ID. Unknown flags are not fresh evidence. Never extrapolate from one side or skip a failed scheduled match to find a later convenient anchor.
- Keep the complete bracket in `WindowObs.segment_bounds(index)`, with no replay, cut, calibration/coordinate-epoch change, geometry loss, ambiguous raw identity or missing current episode along the interval. Raw visible counts matter: two mature episodes cannot hide an extra pending/unresolved person. Initially require the complete expected two/four unique target-court competitors across the bracket; do not manufacture a missing doubles partner.
- Require finite, nondegenerate boxes and finite selected-court foot projections at both anchors; retain current court/runoff restrictions. Bound temporal span, displacement and size change with named policy/configuration. Interpolation is in the existing stabilized reference-pixel/court-metre contexts only. It is a foot/body-position estimate, never an airborne shuttle position or 3D measurement.
- Estimate all competing eligible episodes for the same query and recheck the existing conservative proximity ambiguity policy. Conflicting or overlapping partner evidence must abstain even if carried boxes had an apparent winner. Preserve current observed wrist/racket samples separately; never interpolate those observations or use a reconstructed head point as equipment evidence.
- Supply the result only through an explicitly selected classifier-context policy. Leave the original contact actor and original `participant_hypothesis` intact; record the additional estimate separately, for example `classification_context_source=bracketed_track_episode`, `classification_context_detector_scope=bracketing_person_matches`, `hitter_position_kind=estimated`, and source frames/method. `StrokeContext.extra` can carry this provenance without pretending the estimate is a current detector observation. Initially keep `contact_above_head` unknown when only an estimated box supports that comparison. If bracket endpoints straddle a front/mid/rear decision boundary, preserve uncertainty instead of claiming that a linear point proves the zone.

## Concrete proposed component contracts

These are integration proposals, not new exported types or an instruction to add a second engine. Use the existing host/plugin component contracts where equivalent fields already exist. Keep the current pure `WindowObs` and existing racket detector boundary rather than passing databases or model loaders into the cue functions.

### Contact component

`ContactInference.detect(window: WindowObs, *, start: int, stop: int, match_format: Literal['singles', 'doubles', 'unknown']) -> ContactInferenceResult` consumes only aligned proxy-frame observations. `start`/`stop` remain relative half-open output bounds, with the caller supplying declared contextual look-back/look-ahead. `ContactInferenceResult` carries candidates plus optional bounded gate diagnostics. Baseline fusion is one implementation; gap interval and onset support are additional explicitly configured implementations/cues. Selection/configuration/version are injected by the badminton plugin, and the existing engine remains the consumer.

A candidate needs the current `ContactCandidate` fields plus the following explicit semantics, either as typed fields or structured evidence:

| Field | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| `timing_interval: tuple[int, int]` | Inclusive canonical proxy-frame bounds. Ordinary exact-frame baseline has `(frame, frame)`; a gap hypothesis keeps the unresolved interval. Validate bounds and scene membership. |
| `frame: int` | Existing representative event/display frame. For a gap it is an estimated anchor inside the interval, never a claim of image-observed impact. The interval and method must survive persistence and review. |
| `method` / `version` / `parameter_hash` | Distinguish baseline fusion, `short_gap_velocity_v1` and `observed_motion_onset_v1`; no hidden threshold or format selection. |
| `shuttle_xy` and `shuttle_position_kind` | Existing optional coordinates with `observed`, `reconstructed` or `unknown`. A missing-interval candidate should normally leave coordinates null. An optional fitted point must be explicitly reconstructed. |
| `source_frames` | Original model-observed incoming/outgoing, wrist and optional racket sample frames; frame numbers must identify actual provenance rather than an inpainted trajectory. Bound their count. |
| `participant_hypothesis` | Provisional episode ID, track ID and birth-event ID or explicit unresolved state. Interval overlap alone cannot create a named actor. Competing episode evidence remains available. |
| `support` and `components` | Uncalibrated support and observed/derived cue values. Missing cue is absent, not a measured zero when the distinction affects interpretation. |
| `contact_status` | Always estimated for these components. A later review is separate evidence; no component sets observed physical contact. |

Gap cue configuration should declare maximum gap in seconds, required observed samples on each side, body/equipment support policy, minimum motion-change support, declared contextual radius and supported formats. It must inspect **original observed** masks and reject discontinuities. Onset configuration should declare stationary-context duration, sustained-motion duration and displacement thresholds normalized for body/image scale and time. It cannot infer `is_serve=True` from first visible motion alone. Both can return diagnostics/unknown without publishing a stroke. Initial deployment should be shadow-only until baseline and independent label evidence justify publication.

Pure cue regressions should use literal observed sample frames and expected timing intervals, not compute expectations with the candidate function. A missing-frame gap test should assert an interval plus null coordinates/actor, unchanged observation masks, method/version and original anchors. A dense baseline test should assert exact candidate equivalence when optional cues are disabled. A stitched partition test must produce the same intervals/deduplication as the whole window, including a candidate crossing the output boundary. Negative controls need cut/replay/epoch changes, excessive gap, stationary trajectories, a lone adjacent person, ambiguous partners, reconstructed-only anchors and absent scheduled pose. Onset controls need existing moving flight at clip start, hand toss, pickup, no stationary precontext and no credible body cue. Run all cases in singles and doubles; unknown format must not inherit singles alternation.

### Format-understanding component

`FormatUnderstanding.update(observation: FormatObservation) -> FormatDecision` is stateful and badminton-specific. An observation contains canonical frame, selected-court/geometry epoch, camera scene, actual person-detector execution, current unique detector matches with bounded target-court foot geometry, episode/birth evidence when available, and optional explicit user format. Carried predictions alone cannot count as fresh format evidence. A bootstrap detector capacity must permit at least four players plus bounded uncertain candidates before format resolves.

The output carries `format: singles | doubles | unknown`, `basis: explicit | sustained_observations | insufficient | conflicting`, source frame interval, observed distinct counts/side split, policy version/configuration and uncalibrated support semantics. An explicit user format wins. Sustained 2+2 evidence can support doubles; the absence of two players during occlusion does not support a downgrade. Sustained one-per-half evidence may support a **provisional** singles decision only under declared main/full-court coverage and uncertainty gates; a brief two-person observation remains unknown. Output distinguishes a hypothesis from user-confirmed format. A transition declares reset requirements and invalidates format-dependent calibration/rules/contact/classifier/cache state before the next consumer window. It does not create a roster/name/team mapping.

Required integration regressions: no explicit format with stable 2+2 observations reaches a four-player-capable decision without earlier truncation; explicit doubles plus two visible stays doubles; explicit singles plus four visible remains explicit singles with conflict diagnostics; one spectator/adjacent court/duplicate ID does not create doubles; missing geometry or prolonged coverage loss yields unknown/hysteresis according to the declared policy; an accepted format transition resets incompatible state and rejects old cache fingerprints. A replacement component with a controlled decision must actually change plugin stage configuration, proving the boundary is consumed.
