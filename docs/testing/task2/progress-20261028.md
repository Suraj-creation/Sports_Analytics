## Implemented safeguards

- A missing essential flight observation, or missing non-serve speed or court position, produces `unknown` rather than a fabricated shot class; a reason is recorded and classification confidence is not asserted. Interpolated shuttle points are not used as observed post-contact speed samples.
- Contact events retain the available temporary tracker ID. Proximity attribution abstains on a near tie or insufficient proximity and records a versioned reason and component scores separately from the contact score. The 5% relative-gap and 0.05 minimum-proximity thresholds are uncalibrated heuristics; supported proximity is not proof of identity. Tied-swing scoring is independent of player order; a distant player's swing is not contact evidence.
- Official current stroke accounting includes explicit unattributed events. A player-name edit preserves additional participant records and metadata. Tracker identity evidence cannot be altered through generic verification or automatically carried across a new analysis by frame proximity.
- The session UI shows unlinked provisional visual-track observations and a two-slot limitation warning. This is not a four-player roster, linked identity history, or individual doubles analytics.
- An internal reviewed-identity ledger validates source observations, interval and epoch bounds, conflicts, source retractions and restart. It is not connected to the API, UI, attribution or analytics. The durable request/runner and recovery requirements are documented in `identity-review-ledger.md`.

## Validation and limitations

A full integration-checkout Python run passed 472 tests with seven warnings. After subsequent formatting, a focused 46-test run passed with seven warnings. The frontend suite passed 58 tests, TypeScript checking passed and a production build succeeded. A separate authenticated preview of existing sessions showed the empty-observation state and the two-slot warning; it did not verify a populated new analysis. The live server was not restarted or upgraded, and existing session results were not reanalysed or overwritten.

`gpu-diagnostic.md` and `three-window-diagnostic.md` document bounded GPU 1 component runs on Test7 and existing singles, doubles and amateur windows. They record candidates, source timebases, hashes, runtime and sampled GPU memory, but neither establish contact/shot correctness nor measure full-pipeline latency. The script deliberately withholds classification without valid court/flight evidence. `contact-attribution-replay.md` records a separate same-cache semantic comparison: most existing contacts were already unattributed under the current identity guard; no accuracy claim follows from those counts.
