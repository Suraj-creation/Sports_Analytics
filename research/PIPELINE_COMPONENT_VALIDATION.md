## Concrete input/output and evidence contracts

These 17 responsibility groups cover the requested component chain; closely coupled transport and presentation outputs are grouped. Generic engine infrastructure owns source/media clocks, decoding, orchestration, component lifecycle, append-only events, columnar tracks and transport. A generic engine or API success does not certify the sport inference.

Current compatibility anchors are **pipeline 4**, **perception cache 21**, browser track wire 2 and selected `rally-evidence-tracklets-v1` **component contract 2** with `badminton-rally-output:v1`. Image coordinates are analysis-proxy pixels, x right/y down, boxes `xyxy`; calibrated court coordinates are metres about court centre, with far-court y positive. An airborne shuttle is not a ground-plane metric point.
