Anthony Sinisuka Ginting, born on October 20, 1996, in Cimahi, West Java, Indonesia, is one of the most exciting and dynamic badminton players of his generation, admired for his blistering pace, sharp attacking style, and fearless mentality on court. Rising from Indonesia’s rich badminton tradition, Ginting first caught international attention as a junior when he claimed a bronze medal at the 2014 BWF World Junior Championships, showcasing his immense potential. His senior breakthrough came at the 2018 Asian Games in Jakarta, where he stunned top opponents, including world champion Kento Momota, en route to winning the bronze medal in men’s singles, a moment that established him as a key figure in world badminton and a hero in his home country. Over the years, Ginting has been a consistent performer on the BWF World Tour, collecting multiple titles across the Super Series and Super 500/750/1000 events, and building a reputation as a player capable of toppling the very best on any given day. One of his career highlights came at the 2020 Tokyo Olympics (played in 2021), where he showcased his fighting spirit and skill to claim the bronze medal after defeating Kevin Cordon, bringing Indonesia another Olympic medal in its proud badminton history. He has also played an instrumental role in Indonesia’s team success, contributing to the nation’s long-awaited Thomas Cup victory in 2021, which was Indonesia’s first in 19 years, a triumph that carried deep symbolic weight for the badminton powerhouse. What makes Ginting stand out is his explosive and high-tempo playing style—he is renowned for his incredible speed around the court, his lightning-fast reflexes, and his ability to launch sudden attacks from almost any position. Despite his relatively smaller stature compared to some of his taller rivals, he turns this into an advantage by staying extremely agile and unpredictable, darting around the court and creating sharp angles that often leave opponents scrambling. His smashes, though not always the most powerful, are steep, precise, and well-disguised, and he frequently uses quick net kills and fast exchanges to put pressure on his opponents. Ginting’s style is often described as fearless and relentless; he thrives in fast rallies and enjoys dictating pace, but he has also matured into a more patient and tactical player, knowing when to slow down the game and build rallies before striking. Analysts often note his ability to disrupt opponents’ rhythm, making him particularly dangerous against even higher-ranked players. Beyond his technical skills, Ginting is praised for his calm demeanor and sportsmanship, rarely losing composure even under immense pressure. Off the court, he is admired for his humility, work ethic, and his role as an ambassador for Indonesian badminton, inspiring younger players to follow in his footsteps. With achievements like an Olympic medal, Asian Games success, and numerous World Tour victories already to his name, Anthony Sinisuka Ginting has firmly established himself as one of the top men’s singles shuttlers in the world, combining speed, creativity, and fighting spirit to remain a constant threat on the international stage.
